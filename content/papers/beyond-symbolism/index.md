**Journal article** · *International Journal of Operations & Production Management*

Under what conditions do environmental, social, and governance (ESG) practices improve firm performance? We examine whether manufacturers’ earlier investments in operational excellence help explain differences in the adoption and effectiveness of ESG practices.

Our analysis covers 3,394 firm-year observations from U.S. manufacturers between 2010 and 2023. We measure operational excellence through the range of operational practices firms adopt and their persistence over time. Firms with a stronger operational foundation subsequently adopt a wider range of ESG practices. The performance gains associated with ESG adoption depend on that prior foundation.
