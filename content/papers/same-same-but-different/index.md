**Doctoral dissertation** · Stockholm School of Economics

Why do manufacturers that adopt similar environmental practices experience different financial and environmental outcomes? In three empirical studies of publicly listed U.S. manufacturers, I examine how firms integrate environmental practices into operations and how those practices relate to one another.

The first study examines operational excellence as a condition for realizing performance gains from ESG adoption. The second examines how the positions of environmental practices in a firm’s network of practices relate to financial and environmental outcomes. The third identifies combinations of environmental purchasing and supply management practices associated with reductions in upstream Scope 3 emissions. Most of these combinations are specific to a given year.
