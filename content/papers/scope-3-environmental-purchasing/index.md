**Conference abstract** · Academy of Management Annual Meeting, Philadelphia, 2026

Which combinations of environmental purchasing practices are associated with reductions in Scope 3 emissions? We examine how manufacturers use these practices together to address emissions beyond their own operations. We apply fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis to 239 firm-year observations from U.S. manufacturers over the period 2018–2024.
