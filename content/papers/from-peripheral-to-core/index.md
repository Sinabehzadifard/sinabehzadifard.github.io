**Conference proceedings paper** · Academy of Management Annual Meeting, Copenhagen, 2025

How do sustainability practices move between core and peripheral positions within a manufacturer’s network of practices? We examine 65 publicly listed U.S. manufacturing firms over 14 years, from 2009 through 2022.

The same practice can occupy different positions over time. In our sample, practices in core positions are associated with higher operational efficiency. The analysis also examines whether peripheral practices strengthen the performance contribution of core practices and finds no evidence of that complementarity.
