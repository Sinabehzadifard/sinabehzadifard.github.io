## How does a repair quote shape willingness to pay?

With Ala Arvidsson and Patricia van Loon, Chalmers University of Technology.

When a shop quotes a price to replace a smartphone screen, does disclosing that the replacement is a second-hand original display change what customers are willing to pay? We examine whether the answer depends on how the shop presents the quote.

In the pilot experiment, participants assessed two hypothetical quotes, one in free text and one on the European Repair Information Form. We varied whether the quotes disclosed the source of the display and held the quoted price and repair time constant. For each quote, participants rated the repairer’s integrity and the perceived risk to repair quality, then stated the most they would pay in Swedish kronor.
